Dayfold

A daily log for people who get asked for things all day. Capture every request on today's page; each evening, Review folds what's still open into tomorrow.

Support

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue at github.com/rockykusuma/dayfold/issues. Please say which device and system version you're using.

Common questions

My entries aren't syncing between iPhone and Mac.

Make sure both devices are signed in to iCloud with the same Apple ID, and that Dayfold is allowed to use iCloud (iPhone: Settings ▸ your name ▸ iCloud ▸ Saved to iCloud). Dayfold's Settings shows the iCloud status. Sync usually takes under a minute but can take longer on a poor connection.

How do I add things quickly?

Say "Add to Dayfold" to Siri, share text or a link to Dayfold from any app on iPhone, or on Mac press ⌃⌥D from anywhere (or use Services ▸ Add to Dayfold).

Where's the evening reminder?

Turn it on in Dayfold's Settings, where you choose the time and days. If notifications are off, Settings shows a button to open the system settings.

Can I lock my journal?

Yes: Settings ▸ Journal ▸ Lock with Face ID (or Touch ID / passcode).

Privacy

Dayfold collects no data. Everything stays on your devices and in your own iCloud. Read the privacy policy.
