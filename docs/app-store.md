## Description [4000]
Dayfold is a daily log for people who get asked for things all day.

Write down every request the moment it lands. It goes on today's page with the time and who asked. Anything you don't finish simply carries forward to tomorrow. Nothing gets copied or lost, and nothing disappears until you decide it's done.

EVENING REVIEW
Each evening, Dayfold walks you through what's still open, one card at a time: Done, Still going, Push to a date, Delegate or Drop. Then write a line about how the day went. Tomorrow's page is ready before you close the app.

PEOPLE AND WAITING ON
Note who asked for what and who you've handed things to. Dayfold keeps track of what you're waiting on and reminds you to follow up when something has waited too long.

JOURNAL
A private journal with photos, bookmarks and a month calendar that shows the days you wrote and how much you got done. Lock it with Face ID.

NOTES
Keep the things you need to remember in linked pages, with headings, lists, checklists, quotes and links between pages, like a small personal wiki.

CAPTURE FROM ANYWHERE
• Siri and Shortcuts: "Add to Dayfold"
• Share text or a link from any app on iPhone
• On Mac, a global hotkey and Services ▸ Add to Dayfold
• Paste a list and every line becomes an entry
• A Today widget for your Home Screen, Lock Screen and Mac desktop

FIND AND ORGANISE
Search everything, add #tags, checklists, repeating tasks, due dates and priority stars. Keep Work and Personal in one place with a simple filter.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your data stays on your devices and syncs between iPhone, iPad and Mac through your own iCloud. Dayfold is open source.

## App Review notes
Dayfold needs no account or sign-in. Data syncs through the reviewer's own iCloud account (any iCloud account works; sync is optional and the app works without it). To try the main flow: add a few tasks on Today, then tap the inbox button (top right) to open the evening Review. Notes and Journal are tabs; the Journal lock is in Settings.
