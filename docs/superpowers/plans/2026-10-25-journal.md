# Journal Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** An Apple-Journal-inspired Journal: entry cards with titles, text, photos and bookmarks; suggestions from your day; month Insights calendar; entries on the Day page, in Search and on the Week page; optional Face ID lock.

**Architecture:** Two additive SwiftData models (`JournalEntry`, `JournalPhoto`) replace `DayNote` (migrated in `Maintenance.tidy`, model kept in the schema). All rules — reflection get/set, migration, dedupe, month calendar, suggestions, journal search, photo downscaling — live in `DayfoldCore` with tests. The app adds `JournalView` (the fourth tab / Mac sidebar item), `JournalEditor`, and small sections on existing screens, gated by a `JournalLock`.

**Tech Stack:** Swift 5 language mode, SwiftUI (iOS 17 / macOS 14), SwiftData + CloudKit, PhotosUI, ImageIO, LocalAuthentication, Swift Testing.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-25-journal-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- iOS 17 / macOS 14, one codebase; Apple frameworks only; no new targets or entitlements (Info.plist key only).
- CloudKit: every stored property defaulted or optional; no `@Attribute(.unique)`; relationships optional with inverses; additive only — `DayNote` stays in every schema.
- Logic in `DayfoldCore` with Swift Testing tests; views thin.
- Anti-goals: no streaks, word counts or scores; never discard writing without asking; journal content never in Share update / Copy, notifications or Services; nothing from the journal visible while locked.
- Copy (verbatim): tab / sidebar "Journal" (`book.closed`); filters "All", "Bookmarked", "With photos"; "N entries this year · N days written this month" (singular "entry" / "day" for 1); "This week"; "Showing <Thu 22>" chip with ×; "Evening reflection"; "Your journal is empty" / "Tap + to write about your day."; "New Entry"; editor "Title", "Write about your day…", "Add Photos", "From your day", "Prompts", "Discard changes?", "Delete this entry?"; actions "Bookmark", "Remove bookmark", "Delete"; Day page "Journal", "Write about this day"; Week page "Write about today"; lock "Lock with Face ID" / "Lock with Touch ID" / "Lock with passcode", "Journal locked", "Unlock", reason "Unlock your journal", `NSFaceIDUsageDescription` "Dayfold uses Face ID to unlock your journal."

## Review Focus

1. **Migration running twice, or on two devices** (tidy on every foreground): the second run changes nothing and no duplicate entries survive dedupe. Test `migrateIdempotent` + `dedupeById` in Task 1.
2. **Entries near midnight** (a reflection at 23:59, an entry dated 00:30 the next day): belong to their own calendar day for `reflection(for:)`, the calendar dots and the Day page. Tests `reflectionByCalendarDay` (Task 1) and `entryNearMidnight` (Task 2).
3. **Months with 6 or 4 week-rows** (Aug 2026 Sunday-first; Feb 2027 Monday-first): grid shape correct, no missing or extra days. Tests `sixRowMonth`, `fourRowFebruary` in Task 2.
4. **A full-size camera photo** (e.g. 4032×3024): stored downscaled (≤ 2048 px long side, JPEG), never the original bytes. Test `downscalesLargeImage` in Task 3.
5. **Locked journal**: no title or text appears on the Day page, Search, Week page or Review prefill until unlocked, and it relocks when the app goes to the background. Owner check listed in Task 6 (not unit-testable).
