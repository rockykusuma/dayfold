Dayfold

Privacy Policy

Effective 6 November 2026

Dayfold doesn't collect any data about you. Everything you write stays on your devices and in your own iCloud account.

Where your data lives

Your tasks, notes, people, journal entries, photos and pages are stored on your iPhone and Mac. They sync between your devices through your private iCloud database, using Apple's CloudKit. That database belongs to your Apple ID. The developer can't see, read or access it.

If you turn off iCloud for Dayfold, your data stays on the device only. To delete everything, delete the app and remove Dayfold's data under Settings ▸ your name ▸ iCloud ▸ Manage Storage.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or crash-reporting services.
  • No third-party code that collects data.
  • No servers: Dayfold never sends your data anywhere except your own iCloud.

Permissions Dayfold may ask for

  • Notifications: only for the evening Review reminder you set. Reminders are scheduled on your device.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: only if you turn on the Journal lock. Your device does the check and tells Dayfold only whether it succeeded. Dayfold never receives your face or fingerprint data.
  • Photos: only the photos you choose in the system picker are added to a journal entry. Dayfold can't see the rest of your library.

Siri, Shortcuts and sharing

When you add an entry with Siri or Shortcuts, Apple processes your request under Apple's privacy policy. Text you share to Dayfold from other apps is saved on your device until Dayfold adds it to your day.

Children

Dayfold isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
